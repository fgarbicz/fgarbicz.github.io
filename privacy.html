Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

lifeorg is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google account and no one else's.

What data is accessed

  • Gmail: reading messages and labels, changing labels (e.g. archive, mark as read), and creating drafts. lifeorg does not send email.
  • Google Calendar: reading calendars and events, and creating, updating or deleting events at the owner's request.

How it is used

Data is retrieved and processed on the owner's own computer, solely to help the owner organise their own email and calendar. Before any excerpt is passed to an AI assistant acting for the owner, personal identifiers (names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers and similar) are replaced with placeholders on the owner's computer.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and the placeholder mapping are stored only on the owner's computer. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with any third party except as described above to provide the tool's functionality to its owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

lifeorg's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
